# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tattoo studio owners, artists, and portfolio reviewers evaluating a premium landing page template. Visitors should immediately understand that the demo can support custom tattoo work, artist presentation, portfolio browsing, and a simple booking flow.

## Product Purpose

Noir Ink Studio is a reusable premium tattoo studio landing page demo. It exists to show how a dark, cinematic, high-end studio website can communicate craft, cleanliness, artist credibility, portfolio quality, and consultation booking without feeling tied to a specific real business or location.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, restrained. The brand should feel high-contrast, artistic, mature, and adaptable for multiple tattoo studios.

## Anti-references

Avoid biker clichés, skull-and-flame tropes, neon nightlife styling, overdesigned gothic decoration, fake local claims, and template-like sections that repeat identical card grids or loud decorative labels.

## Design Principles

- Lead with craft: imagery, spacing, and copy should make the work feel deliberate.
- Stay adaptable: copy should present a demo studio without implying a real operating business.
- Make booking feel easy: consultation and portfolio CTAs should be visible, natural, and consistent.
- Preserve atmosphere: keep the dark monochrome cinematic base, with one restrained accent.
- Polish through restraint: improve hierarchy, rhythm, and interaction details without rebuilding the site from scratch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard-friendly links and buttons, readable mobile layouts, descriptive image alt text, and reduced-motion support for animation.
